AISEHack 2.0 · ANRF × IIT Madras · Kaggle

Polymer Property Prediction

Predicting seven polymer properties (Egc, Egb, Ei, Eea, EPS, Nc, Tg) straight from SMILES, with no external data and no pretrained weights. Team ◢◤ The Invincibles ◢◤.

  • Physics-first pipeline: Bloch's theorem treats each repeat unit as the unit cell of a 1-D crystal, giving closed-form band structure, phonon and optical features.
  • Canonical structural representation, verified with zero feature drift across every SMILES rewrite.
  • Structure-grouped out-of-fold validation tracked the leaderboard to within 0.008.
  • 3rd on Round 1 private LB (0.922), Top 12 in Round 2, Rank 4/10 at the finale. Outperformed PolyCL (RSC Digital Discovery, 2025) on several properties.
