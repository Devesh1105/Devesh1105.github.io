Featured · Materials ML

Invariance-First Polymer Property Prediction

Seven polymer properties predicted from a single repeat-unit SMILES string, in one self-contained notebook. Built for AISEHack 2.0, around two themes: explainability and polymer invariance.

  • Mean out-of-fold R² of 0.918 on grouped 8-fold CV.
  • Ahead of PolyCL on all six shared properties, +0.063 mean R².
  • Traced a CV-to-leaderboard gap to leaky early stopping, then fixed it.

Diabetic Retinopathy Detection
