Invariance-First Polymer Property Prediction

ANRF AISEHack 2.0, Round 3

Predicts seven polymer properties (glass transition, bandgaps, ionisation energy, electron affinity, refractive index, dielectric constant) from a single repeat-unit SMILES string, in one self-contained notebook.

  • Mean out-of-fold R² of 0.918 on grouped 8-fold CV, where every alternative writing of a structure stays in the same fold.
  • Ahead of the published PolyCL baseline on all six shared properties (+0.063 mean R²).
  • Diagnosed a CV-to-leaderboard gap down to leaky early stopping and feature selection, then fixed it.

Diabetic Retinopathy Detection

Medical imaging, deep learning

Grades diabetic retinopathy severity into five classes from retinal fundus images. Compares an Attention U-Net (ResNet50 encoder, attention gates, CBAM) against a CNN from scratch and a Vision Transformer.

  • ViT-B/16 reached 87.85% accuracy and Cohen's kappa of 0.816 on a 2,544-image test set.
  • Handled heavy class imbalance with weighted focal loss and class-specific augmentation.
  • Compared models with balanced accuracy, macro-F1 and McNemar significance tests, not plain accuracy alone.

  • TensorFlow / Keras
